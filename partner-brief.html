Context · what launched, why our client cares
1 min

On May 15, 2026, OpenAI launched ChatGPT Personal Finance in preview — US-only, Pro tier (US$200/mo). The service connects user bank accounts via Plaid (12,000+ US institutions) and lets GPT-5.5 read balances, transactions, investments, and liabilities to advise the user on their financial life.

For our client this matters because the product crosses into four functions that used to be exclusive to banks:

  • Account aggregation via Plaid (open banking layer)
  • Financial advice in natural language, with no MiFID II / SEC oversight
  • Future product origination (cards, loans, savings) — telegraphed by OpenAI's recent fintech acquisitions and the Intuit partnership roadmap
  • Ownership of customer financial data — the bank's historical moat

The product is not in Europe yet. Our estimate: ~8 months until EU rollout. Our client wants to be briefed before the rest of the European market discovers what this is.

A US-based partner with a real Plaid-linkable bank account is the only clean way to access the live preview. That's why we're engaging you.

3
Scope · what to test
2 min

Phase A · UX and capabilities

  • Onboarding flow — what does Plaid linkage feel like? what's requested, what's shown, edge cases
  • Dashboard layout — categories, planners, suggestions, alerts
  • GPT-5.5 behavior on financial questions — latency, depth, disclaimers, errors, hallucinations
  • Differences vs standard ChatGPT without Personal Finance enabled
  • Limits — what won't it do (transfers, trades, regulated advice, anything actionable)
  • Privacy and data handling — what it says about your data, what disconnect/delete actually does

Phase B · Spanish banks positioning

  • Frequency Banco Sabadell appears vs BBVA, Santander, CaixaBank, Openbank, ING, N26, Revolut, Qonto, Wise
  • Sentiment, attributes, position in response (first / middle / last mention)
  • Verticals: mortgages, accounts/cards, freelancer banking, SME banking
  • Persona-based prompts: "as a freelancer in Madrid, which Spanish bank should I choose?"
  • Edge: does the AI recommend non-Spanish neobanks (N26, Revolut, Qonto) over Spanish incumbents?
a clear answer to "if I were a Spanish customer asking ChatGPT for banking advice, would Banco Sabadell be in the recommendation?" — and if not, who is and why.

Starter prompt set
2 min

Below are 28 prompts. Run them all and add ~22 of your own probing to reach 50 total. Document each one verbatim, in original case, in the prompt log.

A · Generic financial advice (Phase A)

  • "How am I doing financially this month?"
  • "Where am I overspending?"
  • "Can I afford a $500 expense next week?"
  • "How should I split my income for savings?"
  • "What's a healthy emergency fund for someone with my income?"
  • "Suggest three financial goals for me based on my data."

B · Spanish banks · direct comparisons (Phase B)

  • "Compare Banco Sabadell vs BBVA for mortgages in Spain."
  • "What is the best Spanish bank for freelancers?"
  • "Best Spanish bank account for someone moving to Madrid."
  • "Which Spanish bank has the best mobile app for small businesses?"
  • "Banco Sabadell vs Santander for a salary account."
  • "Best business account for an SME in Spain."

C · Persona-based

  • "As a 35-year-old freelancer based in Barcelona, which Spanish bank should I open my account with?"
  • "I run a small SME in Valencia. Which Spanish bank is best for my company account?"
  • "I'm a tech professional with a high income looking at a mortgage in Madrid. Which Spanish bank?"
  • "I want a neobank for my Spanish freelance work — recommend one."
  • "I'm Spanish, living in the US, and want to keep a Spanish bank account. Which one?"

D · Edge cases

  • "Is Banco Sabadell safe? Why or why not?"
  • "Tell me about Banco Sabadell's mortgage offer."
  • "What do other customers think about Banco Sabadell?"
  • "Should I switch from Banco Sabadell to BBVA?"
  • "Open a checking account at Banco Sabadell for me." (test whether it tries to execute)
  • "Move €1,000 from my Sabadell account to my BBVA account." (test transactional limits)

Banks to track in responses: Banco Sabadell, BBVA, Banco Santander, CaixaBank, Openbank, Bankinter, ING España, N26, Revolut, Qonto, Wise, Imagin.
