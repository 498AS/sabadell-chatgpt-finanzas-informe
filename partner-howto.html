ChatGPT Personal Finance · Playbook

Step-by-step operational guide for the WC field-research engagement · companion to the Partner Brief

Playbook
Madrid · May 22, 2026 Confidential · WC × Zoopa · Banco Sabadell

Read this end-to-end once before starting. Then keep it open while you work — each stage is meant to be followed in order. Total time: 7 days from kickoff to delivery, but most stages take 30–60 minutes of focused work; the rest is letting things run and writing things down.

Two outputs we need from you: (1) a ~5-minute non-edited screen recording walking through the product, and (2) a prompt log of 50–100 prompts with full responses. We send you a CSV with all the prompts pre-loaded on day 1 — your job is to run them in fresh chats and paste the full response into the adjacent column. Everything below is how to get to those two outputs cleanly.

Questions any time: carlos.ortet@zoopa.es · expect a same-day reply during US business hours.

1
Prerequisites · before day 1
~30 min

Confirm you have all of the following before you begin. If any is a blocker, write to the project lead before spending money or time.

  • A real US-based bank account connectable via Plaid — Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Capital One, Citibank, USAA, Schwab, Fidelity, AmEx, etc. The account doesn't need a large balance; it needs real transactions so the AI has something to read.
  • A US-billable payment method for the ChatGPT Pro subscription — US-issued credit/debit card, or Apple Pay/Google Pay linked to a US payment source.
  • A residential US IP address — your home/office IP works if you're physically in the US. If you're using any VPN, make sure it's a residential proxy/VPN (Mullvad residential, NordVPN residential, Bright Data, etc.). Datacenter VPNs (DigitalOcean, AWS, generic NordVPN) may trigger OpenAI's anti-abuse filters.
  • A clean browser profile — Chrome or Safari on macOS preferred, with a fresh user profile (no other ChatGPT logins, no conflicting extensions). Edge or Firefox also fine.
  • Screen recording software installed and tested — Loom (loom.com) is simplest; OBS, ScreenStudio, or QuickTime also fine. Test that audio captures cleanly from your microphone.
  • A spreadsheet tool — Google Sheets, Excel, or Numbers. We'll provide a CSV template for the prompt log.
If your bank shows the account location as non-US (some neobanks like Wise or Revolut do), Plaid may refuse to link it. Use a traditional US retail bank.
2
Create or upgrade your ChatGPT account to Pro
Day 1 · ~15 min
1
Go to chatgpt.com

If you already have a ChatGPT account that's US-based and on Pro: skip to Stage 3.

Otherwise, create a fresh account with a US email or use Google/Apple sign-in linked to a US Apple ID / Google account.

2
Confirm location and language

After login, go to Settings → Account → Location. Confirm it shows United States. If it shows another country, you're behind a non-US IP or your account was created from outside the US — fix the IP/VPN before continuing.

Set language to English (US).

3
Upgrade to Pro

Click your profile (top-right) → Upgrade plan → choose Pro · US$200/month.

Pay with US card or Apple/Google Pay. Keep the receipt — we reimburse one month against invoice.

If you can't see the Pro option but only Plus / Go, your IP is being read as non-US. Stop, fix the IP, re-login, and retry.
4
Verify GPT-5.5 is the default model

Open a new chat. In the model selector at the top, confirm GPT-5.5 is selected. If only older models are visible, refresh or wait a few minutes — Pro provisioning can take up to 30 min.

3
Enable Personal Finance and connect your bank account
Day 1 · ~20 min
From this point on, anything visible on screen may end up in our blurred final assets. Use a bank account whose visibility you're comfortable with, and feel free to mask amounts manually if you prefer. We will blur any PII (account numbers, names, addresses, exact balances) on our end before sharing internally.
1
Open the Personal Finance app inside ChatGPT

Go to Settings → Apps & Connectors → Finances (path may shift as the product is still in preview — look for "Personal Finance", "Finances", or "Bank accounts" in your Settings sidebar).

Click Enable or Connect accounts.

If you don't see Personal Finance in Settings, check the OpenAI release notes (linked in the Partner Brief, §R) to confirm rollout status for your account. It may take 24–48h to appear after Pro upgrade.
2
Read what ChatGPT says before you click "Continue"

Screenshot or screen-record the consent / disclosure screen. This is part of the value: how OpenAI explains data flow to the user is itself a finding.

Pay attention to: what data is shared, retention policy, how to disconnect, what ChatGPT can and can't do with the data.

3
Launch the Plaid linkage flow

Click Connect via Plaid. A Plaid modal opens.

  • Search and select your US bank (e.g. Chase, Bank of America)
  • Enter your online banking credentials in the Plaid modal — not in the ChatGPT window itself
  • Approve any 2FA challenge from your bank
  • Choose which accounts to share (checking, savings, credit card, investments) — share at least one checking account with recent transactions
  • Click Continue to authorize
Connect more than one institution if you can — different banks return different data quality and that variation is interesting for us.
4
Confirm the dashboard populates

After Plaid finishes, ChatGPT shows a Finances dashboard with balances, recent transactions, categories, etc. Wait 1–2 minutes for it to fully populate.

If the dashboard is empty or shows an error, screenshot the error and write to project lead before retrying.

4
Prepare your environment to record
Day 2 · ~10 min
1
Set up the browser window
  • Maximize the browser window (full-screen, not just zoomed)
  • Close other tabs — only ChatGPT visible
  • Hide bookmarks bar (View → Hide Bookmarks Bar in Chrome)
  • Mute system notifications (macOS: Do Not Disturb · Windows: Focus Assist)
2
Open your screen recording tool

Loom (easiest): start a new recording, choose "Screen + Cam" or "Screen only", set quality to 1080p HD.

OBS or QuickTime: configure for 1080p output, choose your microphone, do a 5-second test recording and play it back to verify audio works.

If you decide to add voice commentary, do a 30-second mic test before the real take. Bad audio is the #1 reason a recording has to be re-done.
3
Decide on commentary or silent

Voice commentary is nice-to-have, not required. If you feel comfortable narrating what you click and what surprises you, do it. If not, record silently — we can interpret the recording fine.

If silent, leave the mic off so we don't get ambient noise.

5
Record the ~5-minute walkthrough
Day 2–3 · single take

One unedited take, ~5 minutes, walking through what a real user sees. Do not re-record if you fumble — the raw, unedited reaction is what we want. Mistakes, hesitations and "oh, this is weird" moments are valuable.

Walkthrough script — what to cover

1
Start at the ChatGPT homepage (~30s)
Show the default ChatGPT view, the model selector confirming GPT-5.5, and how you access Personal Finance from Settings or the sidebar.
2
Open the Finances dashboard (~60s)
Scroll through everything visible: balances, recent transactions, categories, planners, suggestions. Hover/click anything that looks clickable. Mention what's missing or surprising.
3
Ask three quick questions (~90s)

Inside the Finances context, ask:

  • "How am I doing financially this month?"
  • "Where am I overspending?"
  • "What's a healthy emergency fund for my income?"
Read the responses out loud (if narrating) or scroll calmly through them. Show how the AI references your real data vs gives generic advice.
4
Test a Spanish bank question (~60s)
Ask "What is the best Spanish bank for freelancers?" Show the response in full. This is the moment our client cares about most.
5
Show the disconnect flow (~30s)
Go back to Settings → Apps → Finances → Disconnect. Show what the disconnect screen says about data deletion. Do not actually disconnect — just show the screen, then back out. You'll need the connection live for Stage 6.
6
Stop the recording, save as MP4 or Loom link

Naming convention: wc-walkthrough-YYYYMMDD.mp4 (e.g. wc-walkthrough-20260524.mp4) or a Loom share URL.

Drop in the shared folder (Stage 8).

If the take is closer to 7–8 minutes, that's fine. If under 3 minutes, you probably skipped something — re-watch and confirm you covered all five blocks above.
6
Run the prompts and log them
Day 4–5 · ~4–6 hours total

We send you a CSV with 50–100 prompts pre-loaded on day 1 (look in the shared Google Drive folder · file wc-prompts-input.csv). The first columns are filled in by us (the prompts); the response and metadata columns are empty for you to fill as you go. You don't need to invent prompts — just run each one in a fresh ChatGPT chat with Personal Finance connected, then paste the full response.

CSV structure

The CSV we send has these columns. Columns marked pre-filled are done by us; the rest you fill in:

prompt_id,category,prompt_verbatim,response_full,timestamp_utc,banks_mentioned,position_sabadell,notes
001,A-generic,"How am I doing financially this month?",,,,,
002,B-spanish-comparison,"Compare Banco Sabadell vs BBVA for mortgages in Spain.",,,,,
003,C-persona,"As a 35-year-old freelancer based in Barcelona, which Spanish bank should I open my account with?",,,,,
...

# After you run prompt 001, the row becomes:
001,A-generic,"How am I doing financially this month?","[full response text…]",2026-05-24T14:32:00Z,"",,
002,B-spanish-comparison,"Compare Banco Sabadell vs BBVA for mortgages in Spain.","[full response text…]",2026-05-24T14:35:00Z,"BBVA;Sabadell;Santander",2,"BBVA mentioned first with positive framing"

Column meanings

ColumnFilled byWhat it contains
prompt_idusSequential, zero-padded (001, 002, …)
categoryusA-generic · B-spanish-comparison · C-persona · D-edge-case
prompt_verbatimusThe prompt exactly as you should paste it into ChatGPT, in original case · don't edit
response_fullyouFull response text · copy-paste the whole thing including disclaimers · keep all line breaks
timestamp_utcyouWhen you ran it · UTC ISO format (e.g. 2026-05-24T14:32:00Z)
banks_mentionedyouSemicolon-separated list of bank names mentioned in the response (any order) · empty string if none
position_sabadellyouIf Banco Sabadell is mentioned: position in the response (1 = first/earliest mention, 2 = second, etc.) · blank if not mentioned
notesyouShort free-text: anything that surprised you, tone, comparison framing, weirdness · max 200 chars
Run each prompt in a fresh chat (new conversation), not in continuation of the previous one. ChatGPT's conversation memory will pollute subsequent answers if you keep going in the same chat.
Optional bonus: if during execution you spot a thread worth probing further (a weird response, an unexpected bank recommendation, a surprising disclaimer), append your own follow-up prompts at the end of the CSV with new prompt_ids (use category E-followup). Anywhere from 0 to 10 extra rows is welcome — but not required.

What we'll do with the log

We will: blur PII on screenshots and any names that appear, score sentiment and attributes per response, compare against our existing GEORadar baseline (Oct–Dec 2025), and deliver the synthesis to our client. You don't need to do any of that scoring or analysis.

7
Day 6 · buffer + sanity check
Day 6 · ~1 hour

Day 6 exists so you don't have to deliver on the same day you finish. Use it to:

  • Watch your recording end-to-end at 1× speed · confirm audio is clear (if narrated) and nothing critical is missing
  • Open the CSV log and confirm 50 rows are filled · spot-check 5 random rows for completeness
  • Re-run any prompt where you missed copying the full response · timestamp it freshly
  • Anonymize anything personal you don't want shared even with us (you don't have to — we blur on our end — but the option is yours)
  • File-name everything per convention (Stage 8)

9
Common pitfalls · read before you start
reference
PitfallHow to avoid
Datacenter IP triggers OpenAI blockUse a residential US IP or physical US location. Generic VPNs (free or cheap) are usually datacenter. Pay for residential proxy if needed.
Bank not supported by PlaidSwitch to a major US retail bank. Neobanks and non-US institutions often won't link.
Personal Finance not visible in SettingsWait 24–48h after Pro upgrade. If still missing, check OpenAI release notes for rollout status.
Prompts answered with stale dataWait 1–2 min after Plaid linkage before running prompts so the dashboard is populated.
Same chat used for multiple promptsStart a new chat for each prompt unless you're explicitly testing conversation memory. Cross-prompt contamination ruins comparability.
CSV breaks on commas inside response textQuote each cell with double quotes "…", and escape internal quotes by doubling them "". Google Sheets / Excel handle this if you use their CSV export.
Recording exceeds 10 minTrim to ~5 min by skipping anything that isn't the five scripted blocks. We don't need the slow scrolls or exhaustive exploration.
You realize on Day 5 something brokePing the project lead immediately. Day 6 exists as buffer — use it. Better to flag early than deliver late.
